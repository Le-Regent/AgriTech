### 4. ABSTRACT
Agriculture remains the backbone of economic development in many developing nations, with smallholder farmers contributing significantly to food security. However, these farmers face numerous challenges including crop diseases, unpredictable weather, and limited market access. This research proposes an interoperable, context-aware web application that integrates **Gemini AI vision-based diagnosis**, real-time environmental monitoring, and market linkage systems. By leveraging Large Multi-modal Models (LMMs) and real-time web technologies, this system aims to enhance farmer resilience and promote sustainable agricultural practices in the Cameroonian context.
